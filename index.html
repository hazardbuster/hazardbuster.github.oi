Map the risk before it moves.

Hazard Buster is a geomatics lab. We turn satellite images, drone surveys and GIS data into hazard maps that communities and planners can act on.

See our researchWork with us

Geospatial science for safer land use

Floods, landslides and earthquakes follow the shape of the land. We measure that shape, model how hazards behave on it, and publish maps that show who and what is exposed.

Our work supports local governments, researchers and students who need reliable hazard data for planning and early warning.

Focus
Natural hazard mapping and risk assessment
Methods
GIS, remote sensing, UAV photogrammetry, GNSS
Output
Open maps, datasets and technical reports

Research areas

Flood mapping

Inundation modelling from terrain data and radar imagery, with flood extent maps after major events.

Landslide susceptibility

Slope, rainfall and land-cover analysis to rank hillsides by likelihood of failure.

Earthquake and ground motion

GNSS monitoring of crustal movement and seismic exposure maps for buildings and roads.

Change detection

Time-series satellite analysis to track glaciers, riverbanks, forest loss and burn scars.

What we work with

  • GISQGIS and ArcGIS for spatial analysis and cartography
  • Remote sensingSentinel, Landsat and SAR imagery
  • UAV surveysDrone photogrammetry and orthomosaics
  • GNSSPrecise positioning and deformation monitoring
  • Data sciencePython, R and machine learning for hazard models
